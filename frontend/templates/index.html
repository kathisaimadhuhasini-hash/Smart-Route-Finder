A* Search States & Evaluation

f(n) = g(n) + h(n)

Heuristic \(h(n)\) is straight-line Haversine geodesic distance. Because \(h(n) \le d^*(n, \text{goal})\) (admissible), A* focuses directly toward the goal without overestimating.
